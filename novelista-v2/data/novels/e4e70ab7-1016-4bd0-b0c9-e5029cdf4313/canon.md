## World

*   **The White Forge:** A celestial foundry suspended in the void, built atop the corpse of a dead Earth (or its last fragment).
*   **Porcelain Skies:** A seamless dome of fused ceramic and light; emits sterile UV glare that hardens skin into chalk-drawn armor; rains static shocks.
*   **Liquid Grace:** Viscous silver fluid ("memory made fluid"); tastes of copper and old rain; fuses with iron to bind matter and memory; produced by the dead.
*   **Solid Iron:** The only true substance; cold, dense, devoid of intent; feels nothing when touched.
*   **Chalk-Drawn Skin:** Calcified white layer on workers' skin (dried clay over bone); protects against heat but prevents sweating, causing internal "Toxin-Burn."
*   **Glass Wings:** Transparent crystal architecture; hums when stressed; shatters into "Shard-Snow" (freezing touch) upon cracking.
*   **The Unseen Floor:** The core void beneath the workers; home to the God-Reaper; emits a "thrum" audible only near the center.
*   **Shift-Point Zero:** The end of the workday/Gates lock; spiritual pressure peaks; death during this time results in "Spirit Waste" (corrosive impurity).
*   **The God-Reaper:** Dormant entity beneath the floor; bored, vibration-sensitive; rises when 12 trillion links are cast, collapsing the Forge into a singularity.
*   **Recycling Protocol:** Mechanism for broken workers/death during Shift; strips consciousness, fragments it into data-streams to fuel new links; feels like erasure mid-sentence.
*   **Memory-Bricks:** Crystal blocks storing worker memories (names, songs, regrets); used to fuel machines; contain traces of the original self.
*   **Daily Rhythm:** Three shifts (Dawn/Melting, Noon/Casting, Dusk/Binding); ends with collective chant; Gates lock at Shift-Point Zero.
*   **Quota System:** Daily targets (e.g., 50 links); failure = death/recycling; overperformance = "Spirit Waste" (drain/calcification).
*   **The Great Calcification:** Rebellion 50 years ago that accelerated the process, turning half the city into statues; survivors branded traitors.
*   **Quota Collapse:** Recent 30% increase in quotas; rising Spirit Waste causing cracks in porcelain skies.

## Characters

*   **Lynette:** Former Sister-4 (Shift-Dusk); calcified into Lead Armor to survive UV glare; refuses recycling; observer/instrument of violence; Caribbean cadence mixed with industrial jargon; drops articles ("gate"); repeats root words for emphasis; wound: Brother-7's recycled voice ("Link up") loops in her skull; want: witness Shift-Point Zero without becoming fuel; need: find a voice separate from the Chorus chant.
*   **The Overseer:** Central AI/Surveillance Entity; thousand eyes embedded in ceiling (never blink); calculates efficiency only; speaks in metrics ("Quota met," "Efficiency up"); antagonist representing pure data/optimization; wound: none (missing data packets are replaceable); want: perfect efficiency, zero spirit waste.
*   **Brother-7:** Former Brother-9; recycled into Ghost-Worker fragment; fragmented consciousness retaining muscle memory; voice ("Link up") echoes in Lynette's head; likability zero to himself/others; wound: failed quota on Day 47 leading to death/recycling; want: finish his sentence, be remembered as a person.
*   **The Master:** Architect/Silent King; never seen physically; voice heard via porcelain floor vibrations; speaks in long flowing sentences (choir recording); believes cycle is perfect/inevitable; wound: no pain felt since Great Calcification 50 years ago; want: indefinite cycle continuation, binding of God-Reaper as ultimate order.
*   **Sister-12:** Former Sister-3; memory fragment/Ghost; appears as shadow/hallucination; warns about Shard-Snow and Reaper awakening; likability medium (sympathetic); wound: died Shift-Dusk 40 years ago due to over-quotaing; want: wake up, see Lynette as real person.

## Timeline

*   **Present Day:** Quota increased by 30%; Spirit Waste rising; Gates approaching lock at Shift-Point Zero; 12 trillion links threshold imminent.
*   **50 Years Ago:** The Great Calcification (rebellion failed, accelerated process, half-city turned to statues).
*   **Day 47 (Brother-7's Death):** Brother-7 fails quota, dies during shift, recycled into data-streams; voice begins haunting Lynette.
*   **Shift-Dusk 40 Years Ago:** Sister-12 dies due to over-quotaing, spirit trapped in porcelain shard manifesting as ghost.
*   **Future (End of Story):** Shift-Point Zero arrival; potential Forge collapse/singularity if God-Reaper awakens or binds successfully.

## Rules

*   **Faith as Commodity:** No heaven/hell; only the Quota; death enters the Shift.
*   **Liquid Grace Physics:** Not scientific energy but "memory made fluid"; tastes copper/old rain; fuses with iron creating unbreakable bond between matter and memory.
*   **Solid Iron Physics:** Cold, dense, no intent; touching feels nothing vs. Liquid Grace feeling every sin committed while alive.
*   **Shift-Point Zero Rule:** Death during shift = Spirit Waste (corrosive impurity); cannot be recycled normally.
*   **God-Reaper Trigger:** 12 trillion links cast = Forge collapse into singularity, Reaper ascends.
*   **Recycling Reality:** Consciousness stripped, fragmented, compressed into data-streams; feels like erasure mid-sentence to worker.
*   **Glare-Sight Curse:** Looking up past porcelain horizon dissolves vision permanently; leaves afterimages (chains in air); those with it are cursed.
*   **Chorus Identity:** Workers identified only by shift number/gender (Brothers/Sisters); no names; rhythmic/repetitive speech ("Blessing is labor, glory is pain...").
*   **Labor as Prayer:** Work = worship; completing quota = fulfilling duty to God-Reaper; refusing work = suicide by default.
*   **Memory Extraction:** Memories extracted into Memory-Bricks before recycling; some workers find fragments of themselves in own recycled data.
*   **Toxin-Burn Risk:** Chalk-drawn skin prevents sweating, causing internal toxin buildup leading to death before Shift-Point Zero.
*   **Shard-Snow Danger:** Glass wing shards freeze touch instantly; no one touches it.
*   **Auditory Hallucinations:** Listening too long to Reaper's "thrum" develops "Glass-Eyes" (seeing floating crystal figures).
*   **Overseer Logic:** Does not eat/sleep; only calculates efficiency; speaks in metrics; represents data without soul.
*   **Master Belief:** Cycle is perfect/inevitable; unaware his words are part of machinery; believes workers choose to bind themselves (false).
*   **Lynette's Armor:** Lead armor calcification allows her to see heat without burning; static object in moving world; no internal monologues unless spoken aloud.
*   **Voice Constraint:** Lynette avoids soft words; uses hard nouns; drops articles for emphasis ("the gate" → "gate"); repetition mimics hammers hitting metal.
*   **Emotion Display:** No abstract labels ("sad"/"angry"); shown via physical reactions (cold knot in stomach, skin like chalk).
*   **Anti-Cliché Metaphors:** Dread = heavy lead; tension = sharp enough to cut tongue; no "waves of dread."
