# STRUCTURAL SUMMARY
The novel follows Kael Valerus, the leader of The Ronin, as they execute an impossible hit inside the Spire for Mr. Johnson. Initially framed as a high-stakes contract where loyalty is a commodity, the Alpha Contract reveals itself as a trap: the "faceless client" hires them to erase a rival while simultaneously authorizing their own erasure as a "line item." As Kael navigates the glitchy physics of Night City and his crew's addiction to Neural Euphoria, he confronts the unreliability of memory and the economic tyranny of the Soul Ledger. The climax sees Kael realizing that the Spire itself is an active data-consumer designed to harvest the Ronin's cyber-arms for Johnson's optimization grid. He refuses the final payment, but loses Rex to the dopamine overdose while fleeing into the Gutter, where the Vultures begin their feast before the rain stops and the city decides who gets to walk out the door.

### Ch 1: The Zero-Sum Line
*   **POV:** Kael (Third-Person Limited)
*   **Beats:** Opening Image (Kael in rain, retinal implant pain); Setup (The Alpha Contract delivered by a voice like grinding bone); Catalyst (Acceptance of the job with negative risk and zero pay upfront); Fun & Games (Infiltration of the Spire, initial success erasing the rival).
*   **Emotional Arc:** Clinical detachment $\to$ Rising euphoria/fear $\to$ Paranoia.
*   **Try-Fail Type:** Yes-but. They think they've won until they see the final line item: their own erasure authorized.
*   **Plants/Payoffs:**
    *   *Overt Plant:* Kael notices the Spire's ads flicker based on wind direction (Thread 4).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Rex comments that "the signal is too loud" (Thread 2 – Neural Euphoria mechanism).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Mention of Soul Ledger debts being unpaid (Thread 1).
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** The Spire lights turn red like a heart monitor; Kael realizes the client hasn't paid yet, and the contract demands payment in blood.

### Ch 2: Blue Light Bleeding into Corneas
*   **POV:** Rex (Third-Person Limited) – Cut to reveal addiction severity through physical action only.
*   **Beats:** Fun & Games continued (Inside the Spire); Midpoint (Discovery of the target's location inside a server farm core); Bad Guys Close In (Johnson reveals he hired them not just to kill, but to use them as test subjects for his new Grid integration).
*   **Emotional Arc:** Mechanical devotion $\to$ Ecstatic confusion $\to$ Physiological collapse.
*   **Try-Fail Type:** No-and. Rex cannot stop the drug feed because it is physically fused to his nervous system; pulling the plug causes seizures that kill him faster than withdrawal.
*   **Plants/Payoffs:**
    *   *Overt Plant:* Rex reaching for wires, seeing "ghosts in wireframes" (Thread 2).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Kael checking his Soul Ledger for unpaid bills (Thread 1 reinforcement).
    *   *Payoff:* Rex overdoses mid-heist; the digital dopamine locks his body, making him a perfect target for Johnson's final line item.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** Rex goes limp, not from death, but from "drowning" in the blue light of Chrome Trigger as he is dragged away by security drones.

### Ch 3: The Gut King's Warning
*   **POV:** Jax (Third-Person Limited) – Cold, corporate tone, interrupted by glitch errors.
*   **Beats:** Break into Two (The trap springs; the rival is dead but they are now "collateral"); All Is Lost (Jax hacks a local broadcast to warn Kael, then immediately lies to save himself); Dark Night of the Soul (Confronting the realization that he sold them out three times already).
*   **Emotional Arc:** Rational calculation $\to$ Guilt masked as optimization $\to$ Desperate evasion.
*   **Try-Fail Type:** Yes-but. Jax tries to escape via hologram but is intercepted by Vulture drones; instead, he offers himself as a "service" to Johnson's AI.
*   **Plants/Payoffs:**
    *   *Overt Plant:* Jax's mention of "optimizing synergy" while typing (Speech pattern foreshadowing his betrayal).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Reference to debts not being paid in credits alone (Thread 1).
    *   *Payoff:* The broadcast is intercepted; Jax is sold as part of the cleanup crew, reinforcing his "Traitor" slider.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** The Gut King's mechanical growl echoes through the neon jungle as Kael tries to flee into the Gutter.

### Ch 4: Uncompiled Reality
*   **POV:** Kael (Third-Person Limited)
*   **Beats:** Bad Guys Close In (Johnson's drones hunt them through the Neon Jungle); Midpoint (Kael realizes the Spire is alive, eating the signal to power its gravity fields); Fun & Games (Attempting to outrun the pursuit using local physics glitches).
*   **Emotional Arc:** Focused rage $\to$ Sensory overload/hallucination $\to$ Acceptance of failure.
*   **Try-Fail Type:** No-and. Kael can dodge the drones but cannot escape the soul debt; the city physically attacks him because he is "uncompiled."
*   **Plants/Payoffs:**
    *   *Overt Plant:* The rain making asphalt slicker than a fresh corpse (Worldbuilding anchor).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Kael's hesitation before pulling triggers, fearing the "rusty hinge" of his spine (Metaphor for Soul Ledger).
    *   *Payoff:* Kael triggers a gravity inversion inside the Spire to escape, realizing the building was designed to consume him before he could leave.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** The rain stops abruptly; the streetlights go out one by one as if powered down manually.

### Ch 5: Receipt Printed
*   **POV:** Kael / Jax (Dual POVs in sequence, switching mid-chapter)
*   **Beats:** Break into Three (Confrontation with Johnson's avatar in the Spire's core); Bad Guys Close In (The Vultures descend on Kael from above); All Is Lost (Kael finds out Rex is gone, his soul sold to Johnson).
*   **Emotional Arc:** Calculated defiance $\to$ Moral disintegration $\to$ Final resignation.
*   **Try-Fail Type:** Yes-but. Kael tries to bargain with Johnson, but Johnson offers him the "masterless" choice: live as a slave or die as a asset.
*   **Plants/Payoffs:**
    *   *Overt Plant:* The dancehall rhythm syncing with security protocols (Thread 6).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Jax's dialogue about death being "just another line item" (Theme reinforcement).
    *   *Payoff:* Johnson reveals the contract was always a gift; Kael is now property, and his choice to stay or go has no consequences because he owns nothing.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** Kael escapes the Spire into the Gutter, but finds the rain replaced by static and smells of burnt copper.

### Ch 6: Leave the Meat for the Rats
*   **POV:** Kael (Third-Person Limited)
*   **Beats:** Finale (The final battle against the Vultures in the sewers); Final Image (Kael walks away, leaving his cyber-arm behind to save power).
*   **Emotional Arc:** Hopeless survival $\to$ Desperate gambit $\to$ Irreversible loss.
*   **Try-Fail Type:** No-and. Kael destroys a Vulture drone but sacrifices his own neural link in the process; he cannot go back to being human.
*   **Plants/Payoffs:**
    *   *Overt Plant:* The dancehall rhythm jamming security systems (Thread 6 payoff).
    *   *Subtle Plant:* Kael's fear of being uncompiled mirroring Rex's death (Theme closure).
    *   *Payoff:* Kael uses the final rhythm to disable the Vulture leader, but realizes his own cyber-arms are now monitored by Johnson's grid; he has been bought back. He walks into a new contract: freedom from credits, slavery from choice.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** The camera pans up to the Spire, which is now dark. Kael's face glows on the neon sign. Receipt printed. No refunds.
