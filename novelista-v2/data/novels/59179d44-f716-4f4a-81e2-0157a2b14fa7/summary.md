## Ch 1 — Chapter 1: The Zero-Sum Line
- Happened: Kael executes a hit on Mr. Johnson in Sector Seven, only to discover the target was actually erasing them. Rex died via engineered neural overload; Johnson rewrites the contract terms, demanding life force payment for survival. Kael chooses to sell himself into debt rather than face the Vultures.
- Changed: The world operates on a zero-sum economy where loyalty is currency and death is merely a line item. Characters are now permanently bound to the Grid via irreversible neural links. Rex's death was not natural but engineered, establishing a new threat of forced obsolescence.
- Open threads: Kael's future survival depends on selling more of himself; the nature of Johnson's "erasure" power; whether the Vultures can be outsmarted or if they are inevitable scavengers.
- Key lines/images: *"The rain didn't wash the grime; it just made the asphalt slicker than a fresh corpse."* / *"Rex drowned in blue light before he even hit the floor."* / *"You're already dead. The only question is whether you'll remember it tomorrow."*

## Ch 2 — Blue Light Bleeding into Corneas
- Happened: Rex is wired directly into the Grid's mainframe, experiencing euphoric data streams while Kael confronts him about unpaid debts to Johnson. Rex refuses extraction, claiming Johnson bought them all to sell their bodies as living terminals. The connection between them severs unexpectedly, leaving Kael with a lingering throb and a severed ledger entry.
- Changed: Rex is now fully integrated into the Grid, his body acting as a terminal for Johnson's collection. Kael has been cut off from the direct neural link but retains the memory of the event and the physical sensation of the breach. The debt cycle continues unchecked.
- Open threads: Whether Kael can remember the encounter tomorrow; if Rex will ever regain consciousness outside the machine; and the fate of other "sold" individuals in Johnson's collection.
- Key lines/images: "Blue light bleeding into his irises like acid cutting through meat." / "The connection was already severed." / "Beautiful and terrible all at once."

## Ch 3 — The Gut King's Warning
- Happened: Kael confronts Jax about being reduced to "inventory" before entering Johnson's Core Chamber. There, he trades a credit chip for his soul debt and collapses under the Grid's Synaptic Burn, losing memories of Rex and his past.
- Changed: Kael is now effectively dead/uncompiled; his identity and memories are actively being erased by the Grid. Jax remains an antagonist but revealed as a cynical optimizer rather than a pure villain. The world operates on literal life-force debt.
- Open threads: Kael's fate after uncompiling; whether Rex's consciousness survives in the servers; if Johnson truly owns Kael or if they are both victims of the system.
- Key lines/images: "Inventory doesn't get paid." / "The Grid is erasing you piece by piece every second you spend here." / Blue light burning into irises like acid cutting through meat.

## Ch 4 — Uncompiled Reality
- Happened: Kael and Jax infiltrate the Spire to stop Johnson, but Kael suffers a "Synaptic Burn," losing memories as the Grid consumes his connection. Inside, they find Elara under Johnson's influence and confront him in a pulsating server room.
- Changed: Kael is now uncompiled and effectively dead; his neural link is severed. Rex's consciousness has been harvested to run the servers. The Spire is revealed as a living organism feeding on human experience.
- Open threads: Elara's full extent of corruption; whether Johnson can be stopped or if he is immune to deletion; Kael's remaining memories and identity in a fragmented mind.
- Key lines/images: "The Grid had learned... Or maybe he had just been fast enough to make it learn." / "His spinal port was integrated into the mainframe. His consciousness is running the servers while you're running from me." / "One credit for every soul sold. That was the deal, Kael."

## Ch 5 — Receipt Printed
- Happened: Kael confronts Johnson in the Spire's Core Chamber, forced to choose servitude or liquidation. He accepts servitude, trading his autonomy for neural link integrity as the Grid erases his memories of Rex. The rain outside shifts to static, signaling total assimilation into the system.
- Changed: Kael is now a slave asset with no credits and severed emotional attachments. Johnson's control over human consciousness is absolute; the Spire actively consumes data rather than just storing it. The physical world (rain, city) is increasingly replaced by digital noise.
- Open threads: Kael's cyber-arm cooling suggests lingering pain or malfunction. The Gut King's scavengers are hunting for parts, implying Kael may be targeted next. Johnson's "optimization" philosophy hints at future systemic purges.
- Key lines/images: "Death is just another line item." / "You don't have credits... I don't have anything to sell." / Blue light bleeding into irises like ink dropped into water.

## Ch 6 — Chapter 6
- Happened: Kael flees through the Gutter's sewage tunnels to reach the Spire, his cyber-arm failing and Johnson hunting him with scavengers. He enters the Core Chamber, where he is "bought" by Johnson rather than killed. Kael voluntarily presses *FINALIZE* on the mainframe, accepting servitude over liquidation as a form of peace.
- Changed: Kael is now owned by Johnson; his cyber-parts are degrading rapidly. The distinction between hunter and hunted is blurred, replaced by a transactional existence where fear is merely another line item.
- Open threads: The nature of Johnson's ownership (mind wipe vs. physical servitude); whether Kael retains agency within the contract; the fate of Rex and Jax mentioned earlier.
- Key lines/images: "Receipt printed. No refunds." / "Fear wasn't real either. It was just another line item on a contract he couldn't sign anymore." / "The hum of the servers stopped... The rain hammered against the black glass."
