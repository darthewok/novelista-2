# Voice

## Part 1 — Guardrails (permanent)
- Third-person limited past tense unless the POV directive says otherwise.
- Body-first emotion; no direct emotion labels in narration.
- No filler adverbs in dialogue tags. Prefer "said" over decorative tags.
- Short sentences can carry shock. Long sentences can carry grief or awe. Use both deliberately.
- Do not moralize. Do not explain the theme in narration.

## Part 2 — This novel's voice
Chosen register: Hard-Boiled Noir

### Exemplar passage — 2 paragraphs taken/adapted from the chosen trial
The rain on Sector Seven didn't wash the grime; it just made the asphalt slicker than a fresh corpse. Kael adjusted his retinal implant, blue light bleeding into his cornea like acid cutting through meat. He saw the contract: zero dollars, negative risk, a faceless voice that sounded like grinding bone eating teeth. *Erase him,* the command read in the matrix, static and cold. *No right to life.*

"Copy that," Kael whispered. His throat felt dry, tasting of copper and cheap energy drink fuel burning hot in his lungs. He was part of the Ronin now, ghosts with cyber-arms and spinal ports plugged into holo-tables screaming prices they couldn't afford. The Spire loomed ahead, a needle in the neon jungle guarded by cameras watching more than recording.

Inside the tower, Mr. Johnson himself stood dressed in a suit that cost more than Kael's entire life savings stacked up in credit chips. He smiled without showing teeth, the skin tight around lips that hadn't moved since he bought his face off. "You have your work cut out," he said, though it sounded like he'd already heard his endgame written in red ink. "The final line item includes... us."

Kael's hands shook. The digital dopamine hit him like a freight train of synthetic joy, a glitch in the matrix short-circuiting his brain with euphoria while his legs turned to jelly. He ran into the rain to hide, but the gutters were full of Vultures now—scavengers born from friendly fire stripping dead Ronin for parts before they could even die properly.

"Where are you?" Kael shouted over the hum of neon signs flickering and dying like burnt-out bulbs. The words caught in his throat as the streetlights hummed and sirens wailed in the distance. Someone had found them. All of them. Not alone anymore. No, they never were when you're part of the Ronin, just another name on a list waiting to be erased.

### Speech patterns per character
**Kael:** Short bursts, interrupted by technical jargon mixed with profanity. Uses present tense for immediate physical sensations (*hot*, *sharp*). Often trails off when fear spikes. Speaks in fragments describing cybernetic malfunctions or environmental hazards.
**Mr. Johnson:** Polite but hollow, corporate lingo used as a weapon. Full sentences, complete confidence. Never raises his voice; the threat comes from the calmness and the mention of contracts and line items.
**Vultures (collective):** Grunts, mechanical static noises, rapid-fire exchanges over comms. Slang-heavy regarding parts and currency. No full sentences usually, more like code snippets or inventory lists spoken fast.

### Prose style specifics
1. **Rhythmic Fragments:** Sentences describing action break mid-clause to mimic the stutter of a glitch or a heavy breath (*The rain fell. The street was wet.*).
2. **Cyber-Organic Verbs:** Nouns are paired with biological functions without adjectives (*blood pumped*, *steel screamed*, *code bled*).
3. **Sensory Overload Prioritization:** Visual and auditory details come first; internal emotional states only appear after a physical reaction has established the mood.

**Anti-exemplar paragraphs (DO NOT sound like these):**
> Kael felt a overwhelming sense of dread wash over him as he realized the gravity of his situation. The rain was beautiful, creating a tapestry of neon reflections across the wet pavement that made everything look vibrant and alive despite the chaos. It was a testament to how the city consumes its own citizens, a paradigm shift in urban existence where loyalty is merely a commodity traded for credits.

> As he walked through the bustling streets of Night City, Kael felt his heart pound with an intense mix of fear and excitement. The air was thick with the weight of anticipation, creating an atmosphere of palpable tension that seemed to vibrate through the soles of his boots. He couldn't help but notice how the neon lights danced around him like a vibrant symphony, underscoring the intricate web of danger that surrounded every corner he turned.
