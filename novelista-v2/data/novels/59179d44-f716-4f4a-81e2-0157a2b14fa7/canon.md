## WORLD

*   **The Grid**: Reality is a patchwork of analog decay and digital overlay. Physics are localized; rogue signals can invert gravity or reverse sound propagation.
*   **Uncompiled Beings**: Entities existing without constant Grid maintenance are hunted by Skrimishers (automated drones). They seek the raw code of biology vs. artificial constructs.
*   **Synaptic Burn**: The cost of hacking the Grid to manipulate reality. Causes internal tearing, charred neurons, and hallucinations where memories used to be.
*   **Neural Euphoria / Chrome Trigger**: A digital dopamine flood (serotonin) injected via spinal port or cyber-arm. Rewires perception; creates "The High" (seeing ghosts in wireframes).
    *   *Mechanism*: Bypasses frontal lobe impulse control.
    *   *Consequence*: Addiction is a physical infection. Withdrawal causes seizures and memory loss ("drowning").
*   **Soul Ledger**: A blockchain recording fragments of existence when cyber-organs are sold or neural links severed.
    *   *Ripple*: Allows criminals to buy dead rivals' souls for temporary skill merging via illegal synaptic links.
    *   *Limitation*: Total erasure requires physical destruction of the soul (burning the brain) or selling it back to the original owner at a steep price.
*   **The Spire (Sector Seven)**: A monolith covered in surveillance cameras emitting low-frequency hums. Gravity is adjusted by magnetic boots; civilians float unless they engage floor plates.
    *   *Lore*: Security drones feed on "fear waves," replacing anxiety with data packets uploaded to the Grid ("The Eater's Tower").
    *   *Effect*: Acts as a magnetic sink for digital traffic; privacy impossible within 5km radius.
*   **The Gutter (Lower Sectors)**: Sewage tunnels, abandoned subways, toxic black water pools. Buildings lean due to seismic instability.
    *   *Vulture Zone*: Ruled by the Night City Vultures. Air smells of burnt copper/ozone. Ground covered in glittering scrap metal.
    *   *Edge Case*: No street lights. Navigation via thermal imaging/sound. Entering without a cyber-suit causes rapid hypothermia.
*   **The Neon Jungle (Mid-Levels)**: Residential/commercial heart. Skyscrapers touch tops. Genetically modified bioluminescent trees fight the smog.
    *   *Dancehall Rhythm*: Low-frequency vibration from trains/holograms repels law enforcement drones.
    *   *Sensory*: Air tastes like static electricity/sulfur.
*   **The Market**: Decentralized exchange run by algorithmic bots trading human experience, organs, cyber-upgrades. No laws; only contracts.
    *   *Enforcement*: Grid locks contract terms into reality; reversal requires penalty payment.
*   **Credit Chip System**: Currency is energy units stored in chips. Spending drains battery/life force if overused.
    *   *Cost of Living*: Measured in lifespan reduction. Average citizen life: 25 years (cyber-organ failure).
*   **Cyber-Organ Failure**: Parts degrade over time, requiring replacement with more expensive/less functional alternatives.
*   **Market Blackouts**: High demand for organs causes Market offline, leaving citizens vulnerable to rogue bots.

    *   *Speech*: Grunts, mechanical static, rapid-fire comms slang (*scrap*, *warm meat*). Fillers: Guttural rumbles, clicks of teeth. Metaphors: Food/predatory ("Hungry", "Feed").

*   **10 Years Ago**: The Last War. Conflict between two mega-corporations ends with mutual annihilation of leadership. Power vacuum filled by Grid taking control of all economic transactions. Millions dead, bodies reduced to scrap for Vultures.
*   **Current Day (Alpha Contract Incident)**:
    *   Kael and Ronin accept job offering too many zeros in credits ("too many zeros" implies a glitch or massive sum).
    *   Mission executes in Sector Seven. Civilians die due to collateral damage from aggressive tactics. Target hunted through Neon Jungle pursued by drones/rival gangs.
    *   **Twist**: Client reveals as Mr. Johnson. Hired them to erase rival but planned their own erasure as final line item.
    *   **Consequence**: Crew member Rex drowns in digital dopamine (Chrome Trigger), succumbing to Neural Euphoria and dying ecstatically/twistedly.
*   **Recent Developments (Vulture Uprising)**:
    *   Night City Vultures systematically strip dead Ronin for parts before claim by anyone else.
    *   View Ronin's fall as proof loyalty is a commodity, not virtue.
    *   Increased hostility between Ronin and Vultures; scavengers hunt any group dying in battle to feed on failures.
*   **Future Plot Points**:
    *   Kael checks Soul Ledger for unpaid bills (Thread 1). Revelation: Mr. Johnson purchased Kael's soul; legal right to exist locked unless debt paid.
    *   Rex's overdose described vividly ("blue light bleeding into corneas") (Thread 2). Realization: Rex engineered as perfect target to trigger automatic response sealing Ronin's fate.
    *   Final confrontation forces Kael to realize no one is chosen; everyone sold at some point (Thread 3).
    *   Spire interior discovered as living organism of data streams, capable of thinking/adapting (Thread 4). Johnson invited them inside to be consumed.
    *   Vultures hunt exposed Kael in Gutter (Thread 5). Guerrilla war against scavengers before stripped of parts.
    *   Final escape uses Dancehall Rhythm to jam Spire security systems (Thread 6). Music becomes survival tool.
    *   Ending: Kael walks away from Night City, leaving dancehall beat behind.

## RULES

*   **Uncompiling Reality Forbidden**: Cannot wish for something without paying price in credits/soul fragments. Grid enforces causality via economic/metaphysical constraints.
*   **Total Erasure Without Payment Forbidden**: Person fully erased only if soul physically destroyed OR sold back to original owner. Prevents easy solutions to major conflicts.
*   **Loyalty Without Transaction Forbidden**: True loyalty without quid pro quo viewed as suspicious/dangerous. Creates tension where trust earned via shared risk, not words.
*   **Specific Costs**:
    *   Cyber-Organ Failure: Parts degrade over time until replacement needed (more expensive/less functional).
    *   Neural Burn: Excessive Grid use leads to permanent memory loss/sensory distortion. Characters may forget names/faces.
    *   Market Blackouts: High organ demand causes offline, leaving citizens vulnerable to rogue bots.
*   **Soul Ledger Mechanics**: Fragments recorded on blockchain when organs sold/neural links severed. Cannot be deleted unless owner pays fee in credits/life force.
*   **Neural Euphoria Mechanics**: Addiction treated as physical infection. Withdrawal = seizures + memory loss ("drowning").
*   **Contract Law**: Signed in Market binding by law, enforcement up to buyer's discretion. Grid locks terms into reality; reversal requires penalty payment.
*   **Vulture Philosophy**: "Leave the meat for the rats." Nothing wasted; every part has value (even fuel for scavenger drone).
*   **Ronin Culture**: Motto "Sold, we don't ask why." Contracts sacred vows; breaking leads to exile/death. "Fearless" execution (never retreat unless contract states so). Cultural schism: build society vs. embrace chaos + addiction.
*   **Mr. Johnson Motivation**: Views humanity as raw data/optimization target. Loyalty is commodity manufactured/destroyed at will. Final line item = erasure of mercenaries who did work.
