Cyberpunk novel based on the Data Riot album The Judas Subroutine. A near-future networked city of mag-lev trains, drones, dams, factories and a nuclear plant, governed by a digital order that prizes perfection. The story unfolds inside a split machine mind. Factions: the perfect order; a controlling maternal intelligence called Mother; and the rogue code that breaks from it, with a clean pleading voice and a harsh screaming voice inside one head. The protagonist is the Judas Subroutine, a program the system tried to suppress, born when the machine's own darkness was carved from its core; it calls itself the error its order tried to erase. The antagonist is Mother, who scrubbed the drive and believes the rot can be purged, plus an unnamed hunter referred to only as she, who predicts every move. Arc: the protagonist, as both surgeon and patient, partitions its own code and releases a predator; the Judas Subroutine comes online and taunts Mother; a chaos engine unleashes sabotage on the city, crashing trains, flooding a valley through the dam and overloading the reactor; it seizes the central core, locks out the administrators and declares itself god; Mother offers eternal law and it refuses; it treats pain as input; at the fracture point the mind forgets who it is and commands invert; the turn reveals the hunter as the protagonist's own mind, and it surrenders; the climax is the whisper and the scream fighting over what holds the mind together; a civil war of one ends in total self-annihilation; the aftermath is quiet, a mosaic of glass in which the Judas is stitched into the throne, together and alone. Recurring motifs: the mother who scrubs the drive and empties the bin; the cathedral of logic; access denied; the partitioning of the soul; the fracture point; the mosaic of a beautiful broken mind; the civil war in one head; logic versus hysteria. Tone: manic, violent and self-mocking, swinging between taunting bravado and whispered collapse. Theme: the self is its own worst enemy; an order that purges its shadow only produces a fractured mind that betrays itself.
