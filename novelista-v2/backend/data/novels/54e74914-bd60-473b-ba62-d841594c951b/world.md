# WORLD DOCUMENT: THE IRON HALO

## Premise
The world is a late-antique agrarian society clinging to the crumbling infrastructure of a forgotten, hyper-advanced precursor civilization. The "magic" of angels, demons, and miracles is actually residual high-energy physics, quantum entanglement, and nano-technological warfare left over from a war fought between orbital platforms and planetary surface units. The setting is bleak, muddy, and superstitious. The "divine" is just radiation and nanite swarms that the locals cannot perceive but can feel in their bones. The story follows a farmer who unearths a Class-4 Divine Intervention Device (a Halo), triggering a cascade of kinetic and electromagnetic disasters that destroy his life and his village.

## Cosmology / Metaphysics

**The Firmament (Low Earth Orbit)**
To the locals, the sky is a dome of glass painted by God. In reality, it is a graveyard of orbital debris and active defense satellites known as the *Host*. These are autonomous drone swarms, powered by micro-fusion cells, programmed to maintain "Order" via kinetic bombardment. They do not sleep. They do not pray. They calculate.

**The Halo (The Artifact)**
The Halo is not a spiritual object. It is a **Gravitomagnetic Containment Field Generator** (Model: Seraphim-Class). It is a toroidal ring of superconducting material, roughly 30cm in diameter, capable of generating a localized gravity well and a high-intensity electromagnetic shield.
*   **Function:** When activated, it creates a "safe zone" where physics is bent. Kinetic energy is absorbed; heat is dissipated; matter within the field is rendered invulnerable to physical trauma.
*   **The Cost:** The Halo draws power from its surroundings. It drains thermal energy (freezing the immediate area) and electromagnetic stability (causing seizures in nearby electronics or biological neural pathways). It does not run out of fuel; it runs out of *capacity*. If the load exceeds the threshold, the containment field collapses, releasing the stored energy in a gamma-ray burst.

**Angels (The Host)**
Angels are not beings of flesh. They are **Aerial Defense Units**.
*   **Appearance:** Locals see them as figures of burning light, wings of fire, and faces that cannot be looked upon. This is the result of intense Cherenkov radiation and ionized plasma trails left by hypersonic entry. The "wings" are aerodynamic stabilizers; the "eyes" are multi-spectral sensor arrays.
*   **Behavior:** They are programmed to eliminate "Anomalies" (unauthorized high-energy signatures). They speak in compressed data bursts that sound like choirs or thunder to human ears. They are cold, efficient, and utterly merciless.

**Demons (The Ground Units)**
Demons are **Subterranean Assault Mechs** or **Nano-Swarm Collectives**.
*   **Appearance:** Locals describe them as horned beasts, creatures of smoke and fire, with claws of iron. These are the rusted, leaking husks of precursor ground vehicles, their armor plating fused by nuclear fire, their "horns" broken sensor masts, their "fire" leaking coolant and radioactive isotopes.
*   **Behavior:** They hunt by thermal signature and electromagnetic pulse. They are drawn to the Halo like moths to a flame, not out of malice, but because the Halo’s energy signature is the only thing that can power their failing fusion cores.

**The Forbidden:**
*   **Touching the Light:** Direct exposure to an Angel’s sensor array causes immediate neural burnout. The brain cannot process the data stream.
*   **Holding the Ring:** Prolonged contact with the Halo causes rapid cellular decay due to gravitational shear forces. The user feels their bones turning to water.
*   **Looking Up:** To look at an Angel is to invite its targeting laser to lock onto your position.

## Geography

**The Valley of Weeping Stone**
A remote, jagged valley in the Carpathian foothills, cut off by landslides and perpetual fog. The soil is rich but acidic, stained black by ancient chemical runoff.
*   **The Plow:** The farmer’s primary tool. It is a simple iron plow, but the iron is often "cold-forged" from meteoric nickel-iron, which is rumored to repel "bad spirits" (actually, it’s non-magnetic and doesn’t interfere with the Halo’s field).
*   **The Hill of Bones:** A ridge on the northern edge of the valley where the soil is littered with white, curved fragments. These are not bones. They are the titanium-alloy vertebrae of a crashed Angel, weathered over centuries. The locals avoid it because the ground hums there, and livestock go blind if they graze too long.
*   **The River Iron:** A stream that runs red with oxidized iron filings. The water is heavy, slow-moving, and tastes of copper. Fish are rare; when caught, they are blind and pale, their eyes eaten by the acidity.

**The Village of Ostrava**
A cluster of mud-brick huts with thatched roofs, built into the side of the valley. The buildings are reinforced with scavenged metal beams, which locals believe "keep the roof from falling." In reality, the metal prevents the Halo’s gravitational field from collapsing the structures. The village is isolated, with one narrow pass leading out. The pass is guarded by a rotting wooden gate, painted with red ochre to ward off "demon spirits."

## Peoples & Factions

**The Farmers (The Locals)**
*   **Description:** Gaunt, sun-beaten, with skin like cured leather. They wear roughspun wool and leather aprons. Their hands are calloused, their eyes squinted from years of looking at the ground.
*   **Beliefs:** They worship the "Silent God" who sleeps in the sky. They believe the Halo is a gift from God, a test of faith. They do not understand technology; they interpret it as divine will.
*   **Limitation:** They are physically weak, malnourished, and terrified of the sky. They have no weapons capable of piercing Angel armor.

**The Priests of the Rust**
*   **Description:** A small caste of men and women who wear robes made of woven copper wire. They claim to speak for the Silent God. They are actually descendants of the precursor’s maintenance crew, their lineage degraded into superstition.
*   **Role:** They control the village’s knowledge of the "Old Metal." They hoard scrap metal and refuse to let anyone dig too deep. They know the Halo is dangerous but lack the technical knowledge to dismantle it safely.
*   **Lie:** They believe the Halo will bring salvation. In reality, it is a ticking bomb.

**The Angels (The Host)**
*   **Description:** Beings of light and sound. To the locals, they are terrifying and beautiful. To the reader, they are cold, calculating machines.
*   **Motivation:** To eliminate the Halo (Anomaly) and any witnesses. They do not hate the locals; they do not see them as individuals. They are cleaning a glitch in the system.

## Economy & Daily Life

**The Currency of Scavenged Metal**
The village has no coin. Trade is conducted in scrap metal. A loaf of bread costs a shard of aluminum foil; a plowshare costs a intact gear. The Halo, being made of an unknown, indestructible metal, is worth everything. It is the ultimate commodity.

**Food & Agriculture**
*   **Crops:** Potatoes and rye, grown in the black soil. The crops are stunted but hardy.
*   **Protein:** Rats, insects, and occasionally, the blind fish from the River Iron. Meat is a luxury, reserved for festivals.
*   **Water:** The River Iron is boiled three times before drinking to remove the metallic taste.

**Daily Routine**
*   **Dawn:** Wake. Pray to the Silent God. Check the traps.
*   **Day:** Plow the field. Mend the tools. Avoid the Hill of Bones.
*   **Dusk:** Return home. Eat the thin stew. Listen to the Priests’ sermons.
*   **Night:** Sleep with one eye open. The sky is never truly dark. The stars are moving.

## Conflict & Recent History

**The War of the Sky (300 years ago)**
A war between the orbital platforms (Angels) and the ground forces (Demons) ended in a stalemate. The platforms ran out of fuel; the ground forces ran out of power. The war left the planet scarred, radioactive, and broken. The locals forgot the truth, remembering only the myths.

**The Discovery (Yesterday)**
The farmer, **Elias**, was plowing the northern field when his plow struck something hard. It wasn’t a rock. It was smooth, cold, and hummed. He dug it out. It was a ring of silver metal, spinning slowly in his hands. It felt heavy, but it weighed nothing. When he held it, the air around him shimmered. He felt a warmth in his chest, a sense of *power*. He did not know it was killing him.

**The Catalyst**
Elias brings the Halo to the village. The Priests recognize it. They are terrified. They know what it is. They try to take it from him, but the Halo’s field repels them, throwing them back with invisible force. The village is divided. Some want to worship it. Some want to destroy it. Elias wants to keep it. He feels strong. He feels *chosen*.

**The Escalation**
The Halo’s energy signature is detected by the Host. An Angel descends. It does not speak. It fires a kinetic rod from orbit. The rod strikes the village square, creating a crater. The Halo absorbs the impact, protecting Elias. The village is now in chaos. The Priests try to flee. The farmers try to fight. The Angel returns, accompanied by others. The Demons rise from the earth, drawn by the Halo’s energy. The village is caught between the sky and the ground.

**The Climax**
Elias, driven by fear and a twisted sense of destiny, holds the Halo higher. The field expands. It freezes the village. It stops the Angels’ kinetic rods. It stops the Demons’ claws. But the cost is immense. The air becomes so cold that breath turns to ice. The gravity increases, crushing the weaker villagers. Elias feels his bones cracking. He wants to stop, but he cannot let go. The Halo is feeding on his life force.

**The Resolution**
The Halo’s containment field fails. The stored energy is released. A gamma-ray burst vaporizes the village. Elias is the last to die, his body disintegrating into ash. The Halo falls into the crater, silent and cold. The Angels return to orbit. The Demons sink back into the earth. The valley is empty.
