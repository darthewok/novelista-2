## ELIAS
**Station:** Peasant Farmer, Valley of Weeping Stone.
**Role:** The Catalyst. The vessel for the Halo.

**Wound:** The Winter of Ash, age 7. His family froze because they couldn't afford the priest’s "warmth charms." He learned that piety doesn't stop the cold; only heat does.
**Want:** To hold the Halo forever. To never be cold or hungry again.
**Need:** To let go. To understand that safety is an illusion provided by others, not an object held in hand.
**Lie:** "If I hold this ring tight enough, the world cannot hurt me. I am the center of the storm."

**Sliders:**
*   **Competence: Med.** He knows the land. He can spot a fault line in a potato crop from fifty paces. He is useless with abstract concepts or high-tech debris.
*   **Likability: Low.** He is selfish, desperate, and increasingly paranoid. He views his neighbors as competitors for resources, not community.
*   **Proactivity: High.** He drags the Halo into the village. He fights the Priests. He climbs the Hill of Bones. He initiates every escalation.

**Speech Signature:**
*   **Rhythm:** Staccato. Short, guttural bursts. He doesn’t finish sentences; he hammers them out.
*   **Vocabulary:** Earthy, tactile, agricultural metaphors. Words like *root, rot, iron, blood, dirt, weight*.
*   **Filler:** None. He wastes no breath.
*   **Metaphor Class:** Physical resistance and gravity.
*   **Example Lines:**
    1. "It hums in the bone. You feel that? That’s life. That’s the God-sweat."
    2. "Stop shouting. The sky hears loud things. It eats loud things."
    3. "My hands are cold. Give me the ring. I’ll keep it warm for you."

**Relationships:**
*   **Vs. Mordecai:** Sees Mordecai as a thief trying to steal his salvation. Resents the Priest’s authority because it cost his family their lives.
*   **Vs. Kael:** Kael tried to buy the ring from him with a half-loaf of bread. Elias beat Kael with a shovel for the insult.
*   **Vs. Elara:** She tried to warn him with signs. He ignored her, thinking her silence was cowardice.

## MORDECAI
**Station:** High Priest of the Rust.
**Role:** The Tragic Knowledge-Bearer.

**Wound:** The "Great Rust" event (50 years ago). He was a junior technician then. He saw the data logs. He saw the sky falling. He ran instead of fixing the leak. His brother died in the radiation zone because Mordecai locked the gate.
**Want:** To contain the Halo. To keep it buried. To maintain the fragile peace of the village through controlled superstition.
**Need:** To confess the truth and accept his role as a martyr, not a guardian.
**Lie:** "Ignorance is the only shield left. If they don’t know it’s a machine, they won’t fear it. If they don’t fear it, they won’t die."

**Sliders:**
*   **Competence: High.** He understands the precursor language (binary/cipher). He knows the structural weaknesses of the village. He can read the "hum" of the Halo.
*   **Likability: Med.** He is weary, paternal, and burdened. People respect him, but they fear his eyes. He is not warm, but he is reliable.
*   **Proactivity: Low.** He reacts to Elias’s discovery. He tries to negotiate, then plead, then hide. He waits for the right moment that never comes.

**Speech Signature:**
*   **Rhythm:** Flowing, rhythmic, almost liturgical. Long, complex sentences that trail off into whispers.
*   **Vocabulary:** Technical terms corrupted into religious jargon. *Cycle, core, alignment, vessel, sanctum, decay, resonance*.
*   **Filler:** "Listen..." "Do you hear the drift?"
*   **Metaphor Class:** Mechanical failure and decay. Everything is a machine wearing down.
*   **Example Lines:**
    1. "You hold a thunderbolt in your palm, boy. You think it’s a toy? It’s a wound in the sky."
    2. "The wires in my blood scream when you touch it. Put it down. Before the air catches fire."
    3. "We are all just rust waiting to happen. But rust takes time. You are rushing the process."

**Relationships:**
*   **Vs. Elias:** Sees Elias as a child playing with a grenade. Pity mixed with terror.
*   **Vs. Kael:** Kael is a useful thug. Mordecai tolerates him because Kael’s family owes the Church a debt. He knows Kael would sell him out for gold.
*   **Vs. Elara:** He taught her the cipher. He trusts her silence more than anyone’s speech.

## KAEL
**Station:** Scavenger / Enforcer for the Church.
**Role:** The Violent Catalyst. The anti-hero’s dark mirror.

**Wound:** The Night of the Long Knife. His sister was taken by "Sky Spirits" (drones) for "purification." He watched from the barn. He did nothing. He told himself it was for the greater good. Now he is hollowed out, filled only with the need to dominate the weak to prove he isn’t weak.
**Want:** To possess the Halo. To use its power to fly to the Hill of Bones and kill the "Spirits" that took his sister.
**Need:** To forgive himself. To realize he is already dead inside.
**Lie:** "Power is the only thing that stops the shaking. If I have the ring, I am the hunter, not the prey."

**Sliders:**
*   **Competence: High.** Expert in close-quarters combat. Knows how to use scavenged weapons. Good at reading intent.
*   **Likability: Low.** Sadistic, cruel, and unstable. He enjoys pain. He is the kind of man who kicks a dog.
*   **Proactivity: High.** He steals the ring from Elias. He attacks the Priests. He tries to activate the Halo manually. He accelerates the end.

**Speech Signature:**
*   **Rhythm:** Slurred, aggressive, punctuated by laughter or spitting.
*   **Vocabulary:** Vulgar, visceral, bodily functions. *Gut, shit, blood, break, snap, choke*.
*   **Filler:** "Heh." "Bleeding heart." "Pretty."
*   **Metaphor Class:** Violence and anatomy. Everything is a body to be broken.
*   **Example Lines:**
    1. "Pretty little sun-catcher. Bet it screams when you squeeze it. Let’s see."
    2. "You pray to the dirt, Priest? I pray to the knife. One cuts. The other just watches."
    3. "Look at him shake. He knows what it is. He’s just too scared to drop it. Coward."

**Relationships:**
*   **Vs. Elias:** Elias is prey. Kael beats him for fun, then steals his prize. He sees Elias’s desperation as weakness.
*   **Vs. Mordecai:** Disrespects him. Calls him "Old Rust." Knows Mordecai is afraid of him.
*   **Vs. Elara:** He tries to assault her when she intervenes. She cuts his eye with a shard of glass. He hates her for it.

## ELARA
**Station:** Weaver / Outcast.
**Role:** The Witness. The moral compass.

**Wound:** The Fever of ’42. She was sick for three days. Her vocal cords were burned by the high-frequency radiation leak from a buried precursor server farm. She survived, but she can never speak again. She was shunned by her family, who called her "The Silent Witch."
**Want:** To protect Elias from himself. To keep the village from realizing what they’ve unearthed until it’s too late.
**Need:** To be heard. To have her warning accepted before the end.
**Lie:** "If I stay quiet, I stay safe. If I speak, I die."

**Sliders:**
*   **Competence: Med.** Good at weaving (understanding structure). Good at reading body language. Can disarm a weapon quickly.
*   **Likability: High.** She is gentle, observant, and kind. She is the only one who didn’t judge Elias when he was poor. She is the village’s heart.
*   **Proactivity: Med.** She tries to stop the violence through intervention, not initiation. She runs to warn others, but is too slow.

**Speech Signature:**
*   **Rhythm:** Sharp, abrupt. No flow. Just statements.
*   **Vocabulary:** Simple, direct, concrete. *Stop. No. Run. Cold. Fire*.
*   **Filler:** None. She doesn’t waste air.
*   **Metaphor Class:** Textile and tension. *Thread, knot, tear, weave, snap*.
*   **Example Lines:**
    1. *(Signs)* "The thread is pulling. Let it go. Or it pulls you."
    2. *(To Kael)* "Your hands are dirty. Leave the ring. Leave us."
    3. *(To Elias)* "You are not God. You are meat. And the meat is cooking."

**Relationships:**
*   **Vs. Elias:** Maternal. She fed him when he was a boy. She tries to save him now, not because he deserves it, but because she remembers who he was.
*   **Vs. Mordecai:** Respects him, but pities his fear. She knows he knows the truth and hates him for it.
*   **Vs. Kael:** She sees the monster he is. She is the only one who isn’t afraid of him, which makes him hate her more.
