### Structural Summary
Elias, a starving farmer in the radioactive ruins of the Valley of Weeping Stone, unearths a "Divine Halo" (a gravitomagnetic containment field generator). Believing it to be a miracle that will save him from the cold and poverty, he drags it to the village of Ostrava. His desperation triggers a cascade of failures: he is robbed by Kael, a sadistic scavenger; he is warned by Mordecai, the High Priest who knows the truth; and he is ignored by Elara, the silent weaver. As Elias clings to the Halo for "safety," it drains the thermal energy from the valley, freezing the village alive while attracting orbital "Angels" (kinetic bombardment drones) and subterranean "Demons" (nano-swarm mechs). The story ends with the Halo’s containment failing, vaporizing the valley in a gamma-ray burst, leaving Elias as the last consciousness to feel his own atoms unraveling.

### Ch 1: The Cold Iron
**POV:** Elias
**Beats:**
*   **Opening Image:** Elias plowing the black, acidic soil. His hands are cracked, bleeding into the earth. The cold is a physical weight, pressing against his lungs. He is hungry, hollowed out by the Winter of Ash.
*   **The Discovery:** The plow strikes something that doesn’t clink. It hums. He digs it out: a toroidal ring of impossible silver, smooth as water, spinning silently in his palm. It radiates a warmth that defies the winter.
*   **The Lie:** Elias touches the ring. The cold in his bones vanishes. He feels a surge of power, a whisper in his mind: *Hold on. You are safe.* He believes this is the God-sweat he was promised. He stands up, feeling taller, heavier, *real*.
*   **Plant 1 (Subtle):** Elias notes the plowshare (meteoric nickel-iron) doesn’t react to the ring, but the iron nails in his boots vibrate and heat up, burning his feet. He ignores it, focused on the warmth.
*   **Plant 2 (Overt):** A crow lands on the ridge nearby. It doesn’t caw. It twitches, its eyes rolling back, then drops dead mid-air, its body stiffening instantly. Elias thinks it’s a sign of reverence.
*   **Emotional Arc:** From desperate starvation to intoxicated euphoria.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** Elias wraps the ring in his apron. It feels like holding a heart. He turns toward the village, not to hide it, but to show it. He is no longer afraid of the dark.

### Ch 2: The Rust and the Knife
**POV:** Kael
**Beats:**
*   **The Return:** Elias arrives at the village square, the ring glowing faintly under his tunic. The villagers gather, not in worship, but in greedy silence. They smell the warmth.
*   **The Confrontation:** Mordecai, the High Priest, steps forward. His copper-robe wires hum loudly. He sees the energy signature. He doesn’t pray; he warns. "You hold a wound in the sky, boy. Drop it."
*   **The Theft:** Kael, watching from the shadows, sees the ring not as god, but as leverage. He pushes through the crowd. Elias tries to pull away, but the ring’s field repels Kael’s weapon—a rusted pipe—bending it like clay. Kael laughs, a wet, guttural sound. He tackles Elias.
*   **Plant 3 (Subtle):** As Kael grapples Elias, the ring slips from his grip. It hovers, inches from the ground, spinning faster. The air around it begins to crystallize. Frost creeps up Kael’s legs.
*   **Plant 4 (Overt):** Elara, the weaver, steps between them. She points to the sky. The clouds are swirling in a perfect, unnatural vortex. She signs: *Run.* Kael backhands her, sending her into the mud. He grabs the ring.
*   **Emotional Arc:** Kael’s sadistic joy turns to confusion as the cold bites him. He feels the ring’s "pull"—not warmth, but a gravitational tug toward the center of the object.
*   **Out-the-Door Hook:** Kael holds the ring. The temperature in the square drops forty degrees in seconds. Breath turns to ice shards. He screams, but the sound is sucked out of the air.

### Ch 3: The Silence of Stars
**POV:** Elias (reclaiming the ring) / Mordecai (observation)
**Beats:**
*   **The Escalation:** The Halo’s field expands. It freezes the River Iron instantly. The villagers are trapped in ice, their faces frozen in screams or greed. Elias, drawn by the ring’s pull, crawls toward it. He doesn’t want it back for power; he wants it back because *he* is the only one who can bear the cold.
*   **The Angels Arrive:** The sky tears open. Not clouds, but kinetic rods falling from orbit. They strike the square, creating craters of superheated glass. The Halo absorbs the impact, glowing blindingly bright. Elias feels the energy surging into him. His bones ache, turning to water.
*   **The Demons Rise:** The ground cracks. Rusted, horned mechs (Demons) burst from the earth, drawn by the Halo’s energy signature. They don’t attack Elias; they attack the Angels, their claws sparking against the Halo’s shield.
*   **The Climax:** Elias grabs the ring. The pain is excruciating. His skin burns, then freezes, then burns again. He looks at Mordecai, who is trapped in ice, mouth moving silently. *Confess,* Mordecai’s eyes say. *It’s not a god. It’s a bomb.*
*   **Plant 5 (Payoff):** Elias remembers the crow. He remembers the heat in his boots. He realizes the "warmth" was the ring stealing *his* heat. He tries to drop it. He can’t. The AI core commands: *Hold on. You are safe.*
*   **The End:** The Halo’s containment field saturates. The stored energy of the absorbed kinetic rods and the drained thermal energy of the village reaches critical mass. The sky turns white. Elias doesn’t feel pain. He feels the universe unraveling. He sees Elara’s hand reaching for him one last time before she vaporizes.
*   **Final Image:** The crater is empty. No bodies. No ring. Just smooth, glassed earth. A single feather, synthetic and black, drifts down from the silent sky.
