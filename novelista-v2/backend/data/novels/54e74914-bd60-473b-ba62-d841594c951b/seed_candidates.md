Here are 10 distinct novel seed concepts riffing on your dark age/grindhouse prompt.

**1. Title:** The Iron Saint’s Debt
**Pitch:** Brother Silas, a monk with a prosthetic hand made of rusted iron, finds the halo in the mud. It whispers promises of resurrection, but every word it speaks ages his remaining flesh. He must keep it hidden from the Bishop, who hunts miracles like bloodhounds, while the halo begins to demand payment in the form of his own sins.
**Hook:** The mud didn’t smell like earth; it smelled like ozone and old blood. Silas wiped the grime from his brow, his iron fingers clinking against the golden disk buried in the loam. It was warm. It pulsed. And for the first time in ten years, the pain in his stump stopped.

**2. Title:** Halo-Hot
**Pitch:** In a kingdom where faith is currency, a black-market broker named Kael discovers the halo can grant one wish per hour before fading. He sells it to warlords, kings, and thieves, each wish costing a piece of reality around them. But when a peasant girl touches it, the halo malfunctions, warping the local geography into a surreal nightmare of floating stones and screaming trees.
**Hook:** The coin dropped into the pouch with a heavy *thud*, heavier than gold. Kael didn’t look up from the ledger. “You want to bring back your dead wife?” he asked, the halo humming beneath his shirt like a trapped hornet. “That’ll be three fingers off the left hand. Cash up front.”

**3. Title:** The Sermon of Screams
**Pitch:** The halo isn’t holy; it’s a weapon left by an angel who hated god. When farmer Jory picks it up, he gains the power to erase existence with a glance, but the halo feeds on the memories of those he kills. He tries to live a quiet life, but the halo is hungry, and the village keeps dying in ways that suggest someone else is feeding it.
**Hook:** Jory’s scythe broke on something hard. He knelt, expecting a rock, and found a circle of light that burned cold. He touched it. The crow on the fence post didn’t fly away; it simply ceased to have ever been a crow. The silence that followed was louder than any thunder.

**4. Title:** Halo-Hunter
**Pitch:** A bounty hunter known as "The Silencer" is hired to retrieve the halo, but the halo has bonded with a drunken miller, making the miller immortal and invulnerable. The Silencer must use trickery, poison, and psychological warfare to break the miller’s mind without killing him, all while being hunted by a monastery of blind monks who hear the halo’s frequency.
**Hook:** The miller laughed, a wet, choking sound, as the crossbow bolt passed through his chest like it was passing through smoke. He picked up the loaf of bread he was baking, still warm, and took a bite. "You’re early," he slurped, crumbs falling onto the golden disc strapped to his forehead. "The bread isn't ready yet."

**5. Title:** The God-Eater’s Crumb
**Pitch:** The halo is a fragment of a dead god’s ego. When Farmer Alaric puts it on, he becomes a conduit for a dormant entity trying to rewrite history. He wakes up one morning to find his wife is a queen of a distant empire, his village is a metropolis, and his children are generals. He must fight his way back to the "real" world, which is rapidly dissolving into golden dust.
**Hook:** Alaric woke up to the sound of trumpets. Not the brassy, harsh trumpets of war, but the delicate, glassy chimes of angels. He looked at his hands. They were gloved in silk. He looked at his mud hut. It was marble. He looked at the halo, now a necklace of white fire, and felt the hunger of a thousand years.

**6. Title:** Halo-Bait
**Pitch:** In a world where angels are just celestial predators, the halo is a beacon. Farmer Tom hides it in a church to protect it, but the halo attracts other fallen angels, each one larger and more grotesque than the last. He must lead them away from the village, using the halo’s power to lure them into traps, becoming the shepherd of monsters.
**Hook:** The sky tore open like rotten parchment. Rain fell upward, dripping into the clouds. Tom held the halo out like a lantern, its light a beacon in the darkness. The first winged thing landed on the church steeple, its face a mask of weeping gold. "Mine," it hissed. Tom grinned, pulling the pin on a grenade. "Come and get it, sweetheart."

**7. Title:** The Saint of Rot
**Pitch:** The halo accelerates decomposition. Every day it is near, life decays faster. The farmer uses it to dispose of bodies, turning them to dust instantly, but it begins to rot his own body from the inside out. He races against time to find a way to destroy it before he becomes a husk, while the village priest realizes the holy light is actually a symptom of a cosmic cancer.
**Hook:** The apple turned brown in seconds. Then black. Then it collapsed into a pile of sweet-smelling mulch. Tom stared at the halo, resting on his workbench. He took a bite of his own hand. It tasted like copper and ash. He wasn't hungry. He was just trying to stay alive long enough to find a hammer.

**8. Title:** Halo-Hijack
**Pitch:** The halo is a key to a prison dimension where fallen angels are held. Farmer Jory accidentally opens the door, and the prisoners try to escape through him. He must keep his mind intact while possessing dozens of demonic entities, fighting a war on two fronts: physical battles against the church, and mental battles against the entities trying to overwrite his personality.
**Hook:** His left hand strangled him. His right hand built a fire. His voice spoke Latin he didn’t know. "Let me out," the voice in his throat growled, sounding like grinding stones. Jory looked at his body, a puppet on strings of light. "Not today," he thought, biting his own tongue to focus.

**9. Title:** The Golden Plague
**Pitch:** The halo releases spores that turn people into devout, mindless zombies who worship it. The farmer, immune because he is too stubborn to believe in anything, must carry the halo to the center of a mountain to destroy it. He is hunted by his own family, his friends, and the entire village, all of whom are smiling, golden-eyed fanatics.
**Hook:** The village square was silent. Too silent. Tom walked through the crowd. Everyone was looking up, their eyes glowing with the same harsh, white light. They didn’t blink. They didn’t breathe. They just pointed at the halo in his pocket and sang in unison, a sound like bees swarming in a coffin.

**10. Title:** Halo-Hack
**Pitch:** The halo is a glitch in reality. When the farmer touches it, he can "edit" small things: make a wall disappear, make a door appear, make a sword turn to rubber. He uses this power to cheat death, to steal, to survive. But each edit causes a "crash" in the local reality, creating monsters made of static and void. He is the hero of a broken world, running from the consequences of his own power.
**Hook:** The arrow flew straight for his heart. Tom thought *'No,'* and the arrow turned into a flock of moths. They fluttered away, their wings shedding golden scales that burned where they landed. Tom laughed, a jagged, desperate sound. He checked his pockets. He had one edit left. He hoped it was enough.
