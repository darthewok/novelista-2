# The Glowing Meadow

A small hollow between two mossy hills, where fireflies gather at dusk and their tiny songs keep the stars company. At the edge stands an ancient oak with a round door at its base.

## The Moonberry
One slender reed in the meadow grows a single silver berry each year. Its pale light teaches the fireflies their songs. Without it, the fireflies forget the melody and the meadow goes dark.

## Inhabitants
- Hedgehogs live under the oak roots.
- Fireflies live in the tall reeds.
- A mouse named Bramble travels through sometimes with news from the river.

## The edge of the meadow
Mika has never crossed into the open grass. She is small and the sky feels very wide.
