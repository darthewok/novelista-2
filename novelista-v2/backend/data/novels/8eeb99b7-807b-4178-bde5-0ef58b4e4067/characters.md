# Characters

## Mika
A young hedgehog, shy and curious. She knows the songs of the fireflies by heart because she listens from her doorway every night. 
- Wound: got lost in tall grass as a kit; the open meadow terrifies her.
- Want: for the fireflies to sing tonight.
- Need: to trust her own small courage.
- Lie she tells herself: "I am too small to help."
- Voice: asks questions softly; counts things ("one, two, three breaths").

## Bramble
A mouse messenger. Brusque but kind.
- Voice: brisk, practical. Calls everyone "friend". Chews mint leaves.

## Old Fen
The eldest firefly, who remembers when the moonberry was lost once before.
- Voice: hums between sentences. Uses old-sounding words like "long-ago" and "by-and-by".
