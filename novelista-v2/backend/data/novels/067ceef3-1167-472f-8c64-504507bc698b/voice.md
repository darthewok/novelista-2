# Voice

## Part 1 — Guardrails (permanent)
- Third-person limited past tense unless the POV directive says otherwise.
- Body-first emotion; no direct emotion labels in narration.
- No filler adverbs in dialogue tags. Prefer "said" over decorative tags.
- Short sentences can carry shock. Long sentences can carry grief or awe. Use both deliberately.
- Do not moralize. Do not explain the theme in narration.
