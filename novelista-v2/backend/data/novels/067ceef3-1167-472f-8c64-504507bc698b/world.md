# WORLD DOCUMENT: THE ASHEN COVENANT

## Premise
The world of **Orodruin’s Scar** (colloquially: "The Burn") is a post-apocalyptic wasteland where the sky is permanently fractured by the **Great Fall**. Three centuries ago, the Celestial Host did not leave; they were cast down, their bodies crashing into the crust and fusing with the planet’s ley lines. The resulting impact created the **Halo Zones**—radiated, reality-warping craters where physics is optional and divine energy leaks like pus from a wound.

Humanity survives in the **Dust Belt**, a ring of habitable land surrounding the Halo Zones. Society is structured around **Salvage Theology**: the belief that angelic flesh and bone are holy relics that grant power, but also carry the "Curse of Memory." To touch an Angel is to inherit their final moments. This creates a caste system: the **Pure** (those who avoid the Zones), the **Touched** (those who have survived contact but gone mad), and the **Heralds** (mercenaries who hunt Angels for their cores, risking madness for wealth).

The central tension is between **Preservation** (keeping the Zones sealed to prevent further corruption) and **Harvesting** (mining the Zones for power, which accelerates the sky’s collapse). The story follows **Kael**, a low-level plowman who accidentally ingests a shard of a Fallen Angel’s halo, becoming a living vessel for the angel’s dying rage.

## Cosmology / Metaphysics

### The Halo Effect
The sky above the Halo Zones is not blue but a bruised, bleeding red, known as the **Wound**. Gravity is inconsistent within a 5-mile radius of a Halo. Debris floats; blood rises. The air smells of ozone and rotting lilies.

*   **The Curse:** When a human interacts with Angelic matter (bone, feather, blood), they suffer **Recall Sickness**. They do not gain powers; they gain *memories*. Visceral, traumatic memories of the Angel’s fall.
    *   *Symptoms:* Hallucinations of burning wings, speaking in **High Tongue** (a language that causes milk to sour and metal to rust), and temporary paralysis from the weight of falling.
    *   *Limitation:* The more Angelic matter absorbed, the less human memory remains. Eventually, the victim becomes a **Vessel**, a mindless automaton driven by the Angel’s residual will.
    *   *Cost:* Survival requires **Dampeners**—crude devices made of lead and mute-stone that suppress the voice and block visual hallucinations. These are expensive and illegal in many sectors.

### The Wound in the Sky
The sky is a literal scar. Clouds do not form rain; they form **Ash-Drifts**. Ash-Drifts are toxic. Prolonged exposure causes **Gray Lung**, a disease where the lungs crystallize.
*   *Edge Case:* During a **Red Storm**, the Wound pulses. Angels who are still alive (or partially so) can be heard screaming across the continent. This drives many Touched to madness.

### Magic System: Resonance
Magic is not cast; it is *inherited*. Only those with high Angelic resonance can manipulate the environment.
*   **Flame-Tongue:** Speaking High Tongue ignites the air. Useful for burning enemies, but burns the speaker’s vocal cords.
*   **Gravity-Well:** Pulling objects upward. Causes severe vertigo and nosebleeds.
*   **Limitation:** Magic degrades the user’s humanity. A mage who uses too much power risks becoming a **Hollow**, a being with no soul, only echo.

## Geography

### The Halo Zones (The Dead Lands)
Three major zones exist, each with distinct environmental hazards.

1.  **Zone Alpha (The Cinder Pit):**
    *   *Description:* A crater of molten glass and obsidian. The ground is hot enough to blister skin in minutes. Floating islands of rock drift in the updrafts.
    *   *Local Avoidance:* Locals avoid the **Shard Spire**, a needle of black glass that pierces the sky. It is said to be the finger of the First Fallen. Touching it guarantees immediate Recall Sickness.
    *   *Resources:* **Star-Metal**, a conductive ore found only in the pit’s center. Used for weapon blades.

2.  **Zone Beta (The Weeping Forest):**
    *   *Description:* A forest of trees with bark like human skin and sap like blood. The trees scream when cut. The ground is soft, spongy, and absorbs sound.
    *   *Local Avoidance:* The **Silence Pools**, bodies of water so still they reflect nothing. Drowning in them is painless but eternal.
    *   *Resources:* **Angel-Flesh**, a regenerative tissue. Highly addictive. Used by the wealthy to heal wounds.

3.  **Zone Gamma (The Shattered Plains):**
    *   *Description:* Flat, barren land where gravity is inverted in patches. One step forward, and you fall *up*.
    *   *Local Avoidance:* The **Up-Side-Down City**, a settlement built on the ceiling of a massive crater. Residents walk on the sky.
    *   *Resources:* **Feather-Dust**, a fine powder that allows short bursts of flight. Dangerous; too much causes the lungs to fill with feathers.

### The Dust Belt (Habitable Zone)
*   **New Babel:** A sprawling, vertical slum built on the ruins of an ancient city. The lower levels are dark and damp; the upper levels are sun-scorched. The air is thick with ash.
*   **The Silo:** A massive grain storage facility that doubles as a church. It is the only place in the Belt where clean water is distributed. Controlled by the **Order of the Mute**.
*   **The Rust Market:** A black market for Halo artifacts. Here, Touched sell their memories for food.

## Peoples & Factions

### The Order of the Mute (The Purists)
*   *Belief:* Angelic matter is evil. The Fall was a punishment. All Halo Zones must be sealed.
*   *Methods:* Inquisitors who burn Touched alive. They wear **Silence Helmets** that block all sound and High Tongue.
*   *Leader:* **High Inquisitor Vane**, a man who has removed his own tongue to prevent Recall Sickness.
*   *Interconnection:* Their fear of the Halo drives the economy of the Rust Market (contraband trade).

### The Harvesters (The Mercenaries)
*   *Belief:* Power is survival. Angelic cores are the only way to escape the Dust Belt.
*   *Methods:* Heavily armored, using Dampeners to survive in the Zones. They hunt Angels for their cores.
*   *Leader:* **Kaelen the Breaker**, a former Herald who lost his mind in Zone Alpha. He now leads a gang of half-mad survivors.
*   *Interconnection:* They supply the Rust Market with Star-Metal and Angel-Flesh.

### The Touched (The Outcasts)
*   *Belief:* The Fall was a liberation. Angels are not demons; they are victims.
*   *Methods:* They form cults, worshipping the Angels they have absorbed. They speak in High Tongue, causing chaos in the Belt.
*   *Leader:* **Sister Elara**, a woman who speaks in three voices at once. She claims to be the mouthpiece of the Third Fallen.
*   *Interconnection:* They are the primary source of labor for the Harvesters (cheap, expendable).

### The Dust-Walkers (The Nomads)
*   *Belief:* The world is dying. There is no future.
*   *Methods:* They travel in convoys of armored wagons, scavenging the edges of the Halo Zones.
*   *Leader:* **Jax the Rat**, a charismatic thief who steals from both Harvesters and the Order.
*   *Interconnection:* They provide transportation and intelligence for all factions.

## Economy & Daily Life

### Currency: Shards
*   **Shards:** Fragments of Angelic bone. They glow faintly blue.
*   *Value:* One Shard can buy a week of clean water. One Core (a whole Angel’s heart) can buy a house in the upper levels of New Babel.
*   *Limitation:* Shards are radioactive. Holding too many causes **Shard-Sickness** (nausea, bleeding eyes, hallucinations).

### Food & Water
*   **Water:** Filtered through ash. Tastes metallic. Clean water is a status symbol.
*   **Food:** Rat-meat, fungus, and **Angel-Flesh** (black market). Angel-Flesh tastes like chicken but grants temporary strength. Overdose causes muscle spasms and hallucinations.

### Technology
*   **Dampeners:** Lead-lined collars that suppress High Tongue. Illegal to make or sell without a license.
*   **Grapnel-Guns:** Used to navigate the inverted gravity of Zone Gamma.
*   **Mute-Stone:** A rare mineral that absorbs sound. Used in prisons and hospitals.

## Conflict & Recent History

### The Great Schism (50 years ago)
The Order of the Mute declared all Halo Zones holy ground. Harvesters were branded heretics. Civil war erupted in New Babel. The city was split into **Upper** (Order-controlled) and **Lower** (Harvester/Touched-controlled).

### The Red Storm (10 years ago)
A massive storm hit the Dust Belt. The Wound in the sky expanded. Millions died from Ash-Lung. The Order blamed the Harvesters for "provoking the Angels." The Harvesters blamed the Order for hoarding Dampeners.

### The Current Crisis
The Wound is widening. The Halo Zones are expanding. The Dust Belt is shrinking. Panic is setting in. The Order is preparing to seal the remaining habitable land. The Harvesters are preparing to storm the Zones. The Touched are preparing for the **Second Fall**.

## Open Hooks for the Story

### Plot Threads
1.  **Kael’s Transformation:** Kael, the farmer, has absorbed a shard of the **First Fallen**. He is not just hallucinating; he is *remembering* the betrayal that caused the Fall. He begins to see patterns in the Wound that no one else can.
    *   *Hook:* The First Fallen did not fall by accident. It was pushed. By whom?
2.  **The Core Hunt:** A rumor spreads that a **Core of Pure Light** exists in Zone Alpha. It can cure Gray Lung. The Order wants it destroyed. The Harvesters want it for power. Kael wants it to save his dying daughter.
    *   *Hook:* The Core is not a relic. It is a *seed*. It will grow into a new Angel.
3.  **The Betrayal:** Sister Elara (Touched leader) contacts Kael. She claims she knows who pushed the First Fallen. She wants Kael to help her release the Angel’s memory to the world.
    *   *Hook:* The memory will drive everyone mad. Is truth worth madness?

### Character Arcs
*   **Kael:** From passive victim to active agent. He must choose between silencing the Angel (and losing the truth) or embracing it
